Features

Everything your operations team handles.

Built for how logistics SMEs in the UAE and India actually work — jobs, people, partners and costs, connected.

Job cards

Create and manage every logistics job in one place, from first request to final delivery.

  • Draft → ongoing → completed status
  • One card per job, every detail attached
  • Find any past job in seconds

Status tracking

Always know which jobs are pending, ongoing or done — without calling anyone.

  • Live overview of open work
  • No more “where is this shipment?”
  • Clear ownership per job

Customers

A full customer database — shipping lines, traders and companies — with every job card linked to each customer.

  • Complete job history per customer
  • Contacts in one place, not five sheets

Suppliers

Manage suppliers and their costs, linked directly to the jobs they work on.

  • Supplier costs per job
  • See who you work with most

Agents

Manage your logistics agents across the UAE and India. Assign them to jobs and see who is handling what.

  • Assign agents to job cards
  • Workload visible at a glance

Trip & operational expenses

Track every operational and trip expense per job card so you never lose margin on a shipment.

  • Expenses linked to the job
  • Spot unprofitable jobs early

FAQ

Questions about FadeLogistics.

Who is FadeLogistics for?

Transport and logistics companies in the UAE and India that manage jobs, customers, suppliers and agents — especially teams currently running operations on Excel sheets and WhatsApp groups.

How do I log in to FadeLogistics?

FadeLogistics runs in your browser at application.fadelogistics.fadeflow.in. There is nothing to install.

How much does FadeLogistics cost?

The Starter package starts from AED 125 per month (AED 1,499 per company per year) and includes training, workflow consulting and maintenance in the first year. See pricing.

Is training included?

Yes. Full team training, workflow consulting, maintenance, updates and direct support are included in the first year of the Starter package.

Can I add AI automation later?

Yes. When you’re ready, our custom package adds AI automation for invoices, follow-ups, reports and more on top of FadeLogistics. Learn about AI automation.

Is my data safe?

Your business data stays yours — we never sell or share it. Data is stored using industry-standard practices including encryption in transit and at rest. Read our privacy policy.
