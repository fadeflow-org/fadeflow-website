Give ChatGPT a task to do later.

Tell it what you need, choose when it should run, and get the result in ChatGPT.

See the steps ↓

More than a reminder: schedule research, summaries, checklists, and other supported tasks.

The real-life starting point
AED 50

My fine for missing a daily video. So I scheduled ChatGPT to research content ideas for me.

Your task can be something completely different.

01 / Get started

Five steps. One useful task.

Start with what would help you. Let the prompt handle the questions.

01

Open ChatGPT

Start a new chat.

02

Paste the setup prompt

Copy the complete prompt below and paste it into ChatGPT.

03

Answer the simple questions

Explain your goal, the result you want, and when it should run.

04

Review and confirm

Review the task details and confirm them. Make sure ChatGPT shows an actual scheduled-task confirmation.

05

Check Scheduled and alerts

Find the task in Scheduled. To receive alerts, check Settings → Notifications and enable Push, Email, or both.

Free ChatGPT accounts may have a limited number of active tasks and flexible time windows. Exact delivery times and some advanced actions depend on the plan and account. A scheduled task can use only information and tools available to it.

02 / Copy & paste

Your setup prompt

Copy it all. ChatGPT will ask you one question at a time.

The complete prompt
Help me set up a real ChatGPT scheduled task.

Ask me these questions ONE AT A TIME, in simple language. Skip anything I have already answered:

1. What is your goal, and what would you like ChatGPT to do for you?
2. What result should ChatGPT send you when it runs? For example, a reminder, researched ideas, a summary, or a checklist.
3. When should it run? Ask for a time or time window, how often it should repeat, and when it should stop if relevant.
4. What city or time zone are you in?

Use my answers to write a clear task instruction. If my request needs web access, a connected app, or information you cannot access, tell me before scheduling it. Do not promise an action you cannot perform.

Briefly show me:
- What the task will do
- When it will run
- What result I will receive

Ask me to confirm or correct those details. Once I confirm, CREATE the scheduled task using ChatGPT’s scheduling feature. Do not merely write a prompt or say you will remember it. Show me the actual task confirmation, including its name, schedule, and time zone.

If scheduling is unavailable on my account, say so clearly and tell me where to check for the Scheduled feature.

Finally, tell me how to receive alerts. If push or email notifications are not enabled, direct me to Settings → Notifications to turn on Push, Email, or both. Do not claim to have enabled notifications unless you actually did.

After copying, open a new ChatGPT chat and paste the prompt. This guide itself does not create a task.

04 / A little help

Before you go

A few things to check if your task or alerts aren't working.

Where do I see my task?

Look for Scheduled in ChatGPT’s menu. Confirmed tasks appear there, where you can review, pause, edit or delete them. The exact location can vary between the app and the website.

Why didn’t I get an alert?

Check that ChatGPT showed a real scheduled-task confirmation, then open Settings → Notifications and enable Push, Email, or both. On your phone, also make sure notifications are allowed for the ChatGPT app.

Can I set an exact time for free?

Free accounts may have a limited number of active tasks and flexible time windows, so a task may run close to, rather than exactly at, the time you chose. Exact delivery depends on your plan and account.

Can it do anything I ask?

No. A scheduled task can use only the information and tools available to it. Web research depends on accessible sources, and tasks that use connected accounts need that connection and its permissions.
