02 / Copy & paste

Your grocery price checker

Copy it all. ChatGPT will ask for your items and your location first.

The complete prompt
I want to compare grocery prices across supermarkets and find the cheapest way to buy my shopping list.

First, ask me only these two questions:

1. What grocery items do you want to buy? Ask me to include quantities, sizes, and preferred brands if possible.
2. Which city or area should I search in?

After I answer, search the internet for current grocery prices and offers at supermarkets near that location.

Check stores such as LuLu, Nesto, Carrefour, Sharjah Coop, and other available supermarkets.

For each supermarket:
- List every item and its price.
- Mention whether it is an offer price or regular price.
- Compare equivalent brands, sizes, and quantities.
- Calculate the total cost of the complete shopping list.

After comparing the stores, tell me which supermarket has the cheapest complete basket.

Also check whether buying different items from different supermarkets would reduce the total cost. Consider extra travel or delivery costs before recommending that option.

Important:
- Use real, current prices from reliable sources.
- Prefer official supermarket websites and current catalogues.
- Include source links and the date prices were checked.
- Never guess or invent prices, offers, or availability.
- If a price cannot be verified, write "Price unavailable".
- Clearly distinguish live website prices from catalogue prices.
- Do not calculate missing prices as zero.
- If some prices are unavailable, show a partial total and explain what is missing.

Present the results in a simple comparison table and give me a clear recommendation.

The prompt asks for only two answers before it searches: your shopping list and your city or area.

04 / What it checks

What the comparison includes.

Every item, every store

Lists each item and its price at each supermarket, marked as an offer or regular price.

Fair comparisons

Matches equivalent brands, sizes and quantities before comparing.

Complete basket cost

Calculates the total cost of the full shopping list at each store.

Cheapest complete basket

Names the supermarket with the lowest total, not just the best-looking discount.

Mixed-store option

Checks whether buying different items at different stores saves money after extra travel or delivery costs.

Verified sources

Prefers official websites and current catalogues, with source links and the date checked.

Honest gaps

Writes “Price unavailable” when a price cannot be verified, shows a partial total and never counts missing items as zero.

05 / A little help

Before you go.

A few things to know about online grocery prices.

Do I need ChatGPT with web search?

Yes. The comparison uses internet search for current supermarket prices and offers. Without it, ChatGPT cannot check what is being sold near you right now.

Which supermarkets does it check?

The prompt asks for LuLu, Nesto, Carrefour, Sharjah Coop and other available supermarkets near your location. What appears depends on which stores publish prices online for that area.

What if a price is unavailable?

The prompt tells ChatGPT to write “Price unavailable”, show a partial total and explain what is missing. Missing prices are never treated as zero.

Are catalogue prices the same as live website prices?

Not always. The prompt asks ChatGPT to clearly distinguish live website prices from catalogue prices and to include source links and the date each price was checked.

Can I use this outside Sharjah?

Yes. Give any city or area when ChatGPT asks. Results depend on what that area’s supermarkets publish online.

Should I buy everything at the cheapest store?

Not automatically. Check the mixed-store comparison too: a few dirhams saved can be lost on extra travel or delivery costs. The prompt asks for those costs before recommending a split basket.
