Make a profile photo with AI.

Upload two clear selfies. Pick a look. Copy a prompt.

Choose a look ↓See the steps ↓

50 complete prompts · No special commands or settings

01 / Start here

Two photos. One full prompt.

A quick guide

01

Open ChatGPT

Start a new chat. Choose a chat where image generation is available. Free access and limits can vary.

02

Upload two clear selfies

Of yourself or someone who has agreed: one front-facing and one slight side angle. Use natural light, visible face, no sunglasses, strong filters, or other people in the frame. You may add a third angle if available; two are enough to try.

03

Copy one prompt

Scroll to a look you want. Tap Copy prompt on that card, paste the complete text with your photos, and send. You need only ONE prompt per image.

04

Inspect and correct

Check the face, hands, clothes and crop. If something is wrong, reply: “Keep everything else. Correct only [the issue]. Match my uploaded face more closely.” You can regenerate if needed.

05

Save and use it

Use square portraits for Instagram, LinkedIn and WhatsApp DPs; a wide banner prompt for LinkedIn cover. Generated images can differ from your real appearance. Avoid presenting a fictional setting as a real event or workplace.

Image generation availability and usage limits depend on your account. /slash names are not built-in ChatGPT commands; the full prompts do the work.

Input guidance

A clear face. Natural light.

Photo A = clear front selfie. Photo B = the same person at a slight angle. These diagrams illustrate input quality; they are not example results.
