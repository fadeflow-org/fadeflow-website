FAQ

Pricing questions.

How much does FadeLogistics cost?

The FadeLogistics Starter package starts from AED 125 per month, which is AED 1,499 per company, per year.

What is included in the Starter package?

All FadeLogistics features — job cards, status tracking, customers, suppliers, agents and expenses — with unlimited job cards, customers, suppliers and agents. Full team training, workflow consulting, maintenance, updates and direct support are included in the first year.

How is the Custom package priced?

We study your current workflow and repetitive tasks first. Once we understand what needs to be built and automated, we send a quote that fits your business.

Is the price per user?

The Starter price is per company, per year.

Can I see FadeLogistics before buying?

Yes. Book a demo and we’ll walk you through the full workflow for your business.

Which currency are prices in?

Prices are shown in UAE dirhams (AED). For anything else, email sales@fadeflow.in.
