What does FadeFlow do?

FadeFlow reduces manual work for small and medium businesses in the UAE and India. We offer FadeLogistics, workflow software for transport and logistics companies, and custom AI workflow automation that handles repetitive tasks like invoicing, email filtering, WhatsApp lead qualifying and daily reports.

What is FadeLogistics?

FadeLogistics is FadeFlow’s workflow management software for transport and logistics companies. It brings job cards, status tracking, customers, suppliers, agents and trip expenses into one place. See all features.

Do I need to replace my current software to use AI automation?

No. We study how your team already works and build the AI automation around your existing tools — email, WhatsApp, spreadsheets and the software you use today. Nothing needs to be replaced.

Which AI does FadeFlow use?

We build our automations with Claude AI by Anthropic, configured around your business rules, templates and data.

Where does FadeFlow work?

FadeFlow serves small and medium businesses across the United Arab Emirates and India. Founded in 2023, our team works directly with each client.

How much does FadeFlow cost?

FadeLogistics starts from AED 125 per month (AED 1,499 per company per year). Custom packages are quoted after we understand your workflow. See pricing.
