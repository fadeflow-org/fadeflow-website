About FadeFlow · Founded 2023

We Take the Manual Work
Off Growing Businesses.

Since 2023, FadeFlow has helped small & medium businesses in UAE and India replace WhatsApp threads, Excel sheets and paperwork with systems that run themselves.

Why FadeFlow Exists

Most SMEs still run their operations on spreadsheets, WhatsApp messages and manual paperwork. As teams grow, information gets scattered, follow-ups are missed and hours disappear into repetitive tasks.

We started FadeFlow in 2023 to fix that. Our product, FadeLogistics, gives transport & logistics companies one place for job cards, customers, suppliers, agents and expenses.

For everything else, we offer AI workflow automation. We learn your company’s repetitive tasks, then automate your current workflow without changing the systems you already use: invoice generation, business analytics, email filtering, WhatsApp lead qualifying and more.
