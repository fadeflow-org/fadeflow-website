FAQ

Questions about AI automation.

What tasks can FadeFlow automate with AI?

Common examples are invoice generation, business analytics in plain language, email filtering, WhatsApp lead qualifying, document data extraction, quotation drafting, customer reply drafting, payment follow-ups and daily summaries. If a task is repetitive and follows rules, it can usually be automated.

Do we have to change our software or systems?

No. Our approach is to automate your current workflow without changing the systems you already use. Your team keeps working in the same tools.

Which AI model do you use?

We build on Claude AI by Anthropic, set up with your business rules, templates, tone of voice and data.

Will the AI send things without our approval?

You decide. Tasks can run fully automatically, or the AI can prepare drafts — replies, quotations, invoices — for your team to review and send.

How is AI automation priced?

AI automation is part of our custom package. We first study your workflow, then give you a quote that fits the work being automated. See pricing.

How do we get started?

Book a demo or email sales@fadeflow.in. We’ll learn how your team works, identify the repetitive tasks and show you what can be automated first.
